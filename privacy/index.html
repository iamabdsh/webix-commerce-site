Webix Commerce Limited — privacy policy

1. Who we are

Webix Commerce Limited ("we", "us", "our") operates this website. You can contact us at ecomwithwebix@gmail.com or via WhatsApp on +44 7848 454771.

2. Information we collect

When you use our contact or enquiry forms, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. We also collect basic technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) to help us run and improve this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide our services.
  • To improve our website and customer experience.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary to deliver our services (for example, with service providers who support this website) or where required by law.

5. Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies needed for the site to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). To exercise these rights, contact us at ecomwithwebix@gmail.com.

7. Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be published on this page.
