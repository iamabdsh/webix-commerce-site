Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we are asked most about our services.

What is Webix Commerce Limited?

Webix Commerce Limited is a UK-based company providing e-commerce management, truck dispatching and UK property services — all under one trusted company.

Which e-commerce platforms does Webix Commerce manage?

Webix Commerce manages Shopify stores, TikTok Shop and eBay accounts — covering store setup, product listings, optimisation and ongoing account management.

What is included in Webix Commerce truck dispatching services?

Truck dispatching includes load booking, route planning, rate negotiation and 24/7 dispatch support for UK and international operations.

What UK property services does Webix Commerce offer?

Webix Commerce offers UK property sourcing, property sales support and investment opportunities across the UK.
